Overview

Client Follow-up Templates is a digital pack of 12 plug-and-play email templates designed for small business owners who struggle to chase clients without feeling annoying. It gives you the exact words to send when invoices are unpaid, proposals are left on read, or projects stall, ensuring you maintain professional relationships while getting actual replies.

Use cases

  • Chasing a prospective client who went silent after you sent a custom proposal.
  • Gently reminding a slow-paying client that an invoice is past due without sounding desperate.
  • Re-engaging a past client who expressed interest in working together again but never booked.

How to use it

  1. Download the ZIP file immediately after purchase and open the templates in your preferred word processor or email client.
  2. Select the template that matches your specific situation and tone preference (gentle, direct, or casual).
